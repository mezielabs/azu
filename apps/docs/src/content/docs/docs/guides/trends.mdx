"Is revenue going up?" needs a line, not a number. A trend is a widget that counts along a date column in buckets.

<DemoCode file="app/admin/screens/overview.ts" region="trend" title="app/admin/screens/overview.ts (part)" />

`date()` is required on a trend. Everything else in [Widgets](/docs/guides/widgets/) about aggregates, access, ranges, and formats applies.

## Buckets

The bucket follows the screen's range:

| Range | Bucket |
| --- | --- |
| `today` | hours |
| `7d`, `30d`, `90d`, `month` | days |
| `quarter` | weeks |
| `year` | months |

`.by('hour' | 'day' | 'week' | 'month')` fixes it instead, up to 100 buckets.

Buckets are in the panel's timezone. Weeks start on Monday. Days across a clock change are 23 or 25 hours.

Note: `azu:doctor` reports a `by()` that can need more than 100 buckets in one of the screen's ranges.

## Looks

- It's a line by default, or bars with `.bars()`.
- `.compare()` adds the previous period as a muted line.
- `.lowerIsBetter()` works as it does on values.
- It spans the whole grid by default.

## Reading it

- Hover or focus the chart for each bucket's value.
- Use the arrow keys to move between buckets.
- Press Enter or click to open the list, filtered to that day, week, or month.
- "View as table" shows the numbers.

## Speed

These figures are p95, on 100,000 orders on Postgres, with an index on `placed_at`. The demo's revenue trend with `compare()` takes 59 ms over 90 days by day. Over a year by month, it takes 26 ms.
