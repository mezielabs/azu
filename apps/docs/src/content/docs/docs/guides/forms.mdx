## Sections

A long form reads better in groups. Call `.section('Details')` on a field to put it under a heading. It works on resource, screen, and action form fields.

<DemoCode file="app/admin/resources/product_resource.ts" region="fields" />

How sections group:

- Groups follow the first appearance of each heading. Fields keep their declared order within a group.
- Fields without a section form an unheaded group. A blank heading puts a field back in that group.
- A group exists only when it has visible fields the user is allowed to see.
- Headings stay visible: level 2 on a page, level 3 under an action dialog's title.
- Fields stack on phones.
- Validation focuses the first invalid control, in displayed order.

Forms without sections keep their usual layout, and detail pages don't change.

## Save modes

The primary button saves and goes to the record's page. Beside it, a second button saves and stays:

- **Create & add another**, on the create form, saves and opens a fresh form. On phones it reads "Add another".
- **Save & keep editing**, on the edit form, saves and comes back to the edit form. On phones it reads "Keep editing".

After a create, the toast links to the new record when the user may `view` it. That link needs session middleware.

The save bar sticks to the bottom of the window while the form scrolls.

## Unsaved changes

Leaving a form with unsaved changes asks first:

- Inertia visits ask with a "Leave without saving?" dialog. That covers the sidebar, a breadcrumb, Cancel, and Sign out.
- Reloading, closing the tab, or leaving the app shows the browser's own prompt.
- The form's own submit never asks, and neither does what follows a successful save.

Note: the browser's Back button, between two pages of the panel, leaves without asking. Inertia can't intercept it.

## What gets saved

Azu reads only the fields the form showed this user from the request. Read-only fields are left out. Then your validator checks the rest, and only its output is written. See [Validation](/docs/guides/validation/).

`beforeSave`, the save, and `afterSave` share one transaction.
